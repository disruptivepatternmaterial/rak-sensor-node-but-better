# ADR-0010 — RAK19007 `VDD` source: resolved by the schematics

- **Status:** Closed 2026-09-05 — exit criterion (a) met, "a RAK schematic … mapping the relevant
  physical board's `VDD` source"
- **Date:** 2026-08-31 (opened), 2026-09-05 (closed)
- **Tracks:** #102

## Context

The hardware guide previously instructed a coreless RAK19007 to supply 3.3 V from its `VDD` pad
during pack qualification. That was not the setup used for the successful capture: capture 13
used a 3.3 V, 50 mA current-limited bench supply with no base board in the loop
[CITE(bench): `docs/EVIDENCE.md`, capture 13](../EVIDENCE.md).

The available sources appeared to conflict:

- RAK's official RAK19007 datasheet says Core-connector pins 17/18 `VDD` are “Generated by MCU
  module,” and repeats that sensor-slot `VDD` is “Generated by CPU module”
  [CITE(datasheet): RAK19007 official raw datasheet, connector tables — CIT-RAK19007-RAW](../CITATIONS.md).
- RAK forum guidance describes a base-board 3.3 V regulator and reports troubleshooting a board
  with the Core removed
  [CITE(prior-art): RAK forum, “USB-C Charger Broke RAK4631 or RAK19007?”](https://forum.rakwireless.com/t/usb-c-charger-broke-rak4631-or-rak19007/13532).
- A third-party manual describes the charger output feeding a step-down regulator
  [CITE(prior-art): RAK19007 manual mirror](https://manuals.plus/rak/rak19007-wisblock-base-board-2nd-gen-manual).

## Resolution

All three sources were correct. They describe two different nets that both sit at 3.3 V.

| Net | Source | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `3V3` | The base board's own `SGM6036-ADJ` buck (`U3`), fed from `VCC` = USB via `D8` or battery via `Q3`. Always on (`EN` tied to `VCC`). Leaves the base board on CPU-slot pins 5/6. | [CITE(datasheet): RAK19007 power sheet — CIT-RAK19007-SCH-POWER](../CITATIONS.md) |
| `VDD` | CPU-slot pins 17/18 and the edge-header `VDD` pad (`J12` pin 1), plus the 4.7 kΩ I²C pull-ups `R10–R13`. **Whatever the core puts on pins 17/18.** | [CITE(datasheet): RAK19007 slots sheet — CIT-RAK19007-SCH-SLOTS](../CITATIONS.md) |
| on the RAK4631 | Connector pins 17/18 are wired to the **same `3V3` net** that arrives on pins 5/6. The core generates nothing; it loops the base board's rail back out. | [CITE(datasheet): RAK4631 schematic, connector `J1` — CIT-RAK4631-SCH](../CITATIONS.md) |

So, on a RAK19007 + RAK4631:

- `VDD` pad = base-board `3V3` = 3.3 V from the `SGM6036`, **through the core module's connector**.
- Remove the core and the pad goes dead — the jumper is gone, not the regulator. That is exactly
  what the forum posters measured and what the datasheet's "generated by MCU module" was trying to
  say (the RAK19010 datasheet states the intent more clearly: "for power sensor board **if the
  MCU IO level is not 3.3 V**" [CITE(datasheet): CIT-RAK19010-RAW](../CITATIONS.md)).

A second fact fell out of the same drawing and matters more than the first: the RAK4631's
**MCU is not powered from `3V3` at all.** `VBAT → R1 (0 Ω) → VBAT_NRF` (the nRF52840 `VDDH`), and
`R4` from `3V3` to `VDD_NRF` is NC. The nRF52840 runs in high-voltage mode from `VBAT` with its
internal regulator, so the GPIO rail — and the `VDD + 0.3 V` pad limit every safety statement in
this repo leans on — is set by `UICR.REGOUT0`, default 1.8 V unless a bootloader wrote 3.3 V
[CITE(datasheet): CIT-RAK4630-RAW](../CITATIONS.md)
[CITE(prior-art): Adafruit bootloader `boards.c` — CIT-ADA-BOOTLOADER-REGOUT0](../CITATIONS.md).
That register is unread on every core this project owns. Reading it is a non-destructive SWD
word read at `0x10001304`; `HARDWARE.md` § "What the schematics establish" lists it as an open
check.

## Decision

1. A coreless RAK19007 `VDD` pad is **not** a 3.3 V source — because the pad is open-circuit
   without a core, which is now a wiring fact rather than an unresolved conflict. The prohibition
   stands; its reason changed.
2. A **cored** RAK19007's `VDD` pad is the base board's `3V3` buck output and is the correct
   place for the pack's `3V3_In` (pin 4) — always on, unlike `3V3_S`.
3. `VBAT`/`VDD` readings alone still do not identify a failed charger, regulator, base board, or
   core; but the decision tree is now drawable: `3V3` live and `VDD` dead ⇒ core absent or core
   connector not mated; both dead ⇒ `VCC`/`SGM6036` path; `VBAT` dead with USB present ⇒
   `TP4054` path.
4. Pack qualification remains the capture-13 bench-supply setup. Nothing here reopens a pad.

## Exit criteria — met

- [x] a RAK schematic mapping the relevant board's `VDD` source: `CIT-RAK19007-SCH-SLOTS` (the
  pad is pins 17/18) + `CIT-RAK4631-SCH` (pins 17/18 are `3V3`). Board revision on the drawing:
  RAK19007 sheet dated 2022-02-17; the RAK4631 drawing carries no date. If a physical board ever
  disagrees, the drawing loses and this ADR reopens.
